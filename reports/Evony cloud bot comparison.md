# Start With ESB Cloud, Then Verify Everything

No public evidence can prove which Evony: The King's Return bot is "best" or "fastest," but on documented features **ESB Cloud, the hosted version of Evony Smart Bot, is the strongest first trial for both of your goals**. It is the only browser-controlled cloud bot whose marketing describes a real solo-hunting system (named presets, troop tiers, "soloable lists," chaining and stamina rules). It is also the only vendor that documents farm-specific mechanics, including a listed "Resources Transfer" feature, and at an advertised $5 for the first account plus $3 for each extra it is the cheapest cloud option per account. Evony Auto ($49–$149 a month, every automation on every plan) is the broadest all-in-one alternative, and Ragebot TKR documents the finest solo and healing controls but runs only on your own PC. Two game mechanics matter more than vendor choice: **stamina, not bot speed, caps how many monsters any bot can solo per day**, and **Evony's official Family Group feature plus a high Embassy level set how much RSS your farms can move to your main**. Every bot breaks Evony's Terms of Service, the vendors themselves say so, and Evony states it will not restore an account a bot has logged into, so run trials on farm accounts before your main touches a bot. For PvP battle reports the best low-cost method is free: star the report, capture lossless PNGs of its main, buffs and details screens from the Windows client or a high-resolution emulator, and post them to Discord with key numbers logged in a shared Google Sheet. Treat every vendor claim here as unverified marketing, because the researchers could not open vendor sites, evony.com, fan wikis or Reddit and worked from search-result summaries.

## Search snippets of vendor marketing are the entire evidence base

Network restrictions blocked direct reads of nearly every relevant site: the vendor pages for Evony Auto, EvonAI, GNBots, Fbots and Emulator Automation, the Evony Guide Wiki, evony.com and the official forums. Reddit's r/Evony and r/EvonyTKR, the likeliest home of first-hand ban and performance reports, could not be searched at all. **Every price, feature and policy quote in this report comes from search-engine summaries of pages, not from the pages themselves.** That matters because vendors contradict their own sites. Fbots quotes both $1 and $1.50 per account for its PC program and both $4 and $5 for its shared server ([Fbots](https://fbots.org/en/evony-bot)). BoostBot lists Premium at $14.92 a month on its own page and $25 on a sister site, "unlimited" accounts against a 60-account cap, and a 24-hour against a 14-day refund window ([BoostBot](https://boostbot.org/evony-kings-return-bot/); [evonykingsreturnbot.com](https://evonykingsreturnbot.com/)). ESB's payment page sells monthly tiers while its FAQ says there is no subscription and the full amount is due up front ([ESB payment](https://www.evonysmartbot.com/p/payment.html); [ESB FAQ](https://www.evonysmartbot.com/p/faq.html)).

Independent reputation data is just as thin. Only GNBots and BoostBot have meaningful review footprints, and no Trustpilot page turned up for ESB, Evony Auto, EvonAI, iScope, Ragebot or Fbots. The only published ranking, a BestMobileBots comparison updated August 2026 that names BoostBot the "top overall pick," mirrors BoostBot's own copy and reads as affiliate content; it also claims ESB lacks cloud hosting, which ESB Cloud's launch has made out of date ([BestMobileBots](https://bestmobilebots.com/evony-kings-return-bot/)). **No source offers a measured kill rate, RSS-per-day benchmark or ban rate for any named bot.** In this report "best" therefore means *best documented for your use case*, not best proven in play. The final section shows how to close that gap with free trials before you pay.

## ESB Cloud leads on documented solo controls, but stamina caps every bot

The research found 16 bots marketed for TKR, and nearly all lead with boss-rally automation. Only a handful describe genuine solo hunting, meaning one march per kill with control over targets and troops. ESB Cloud's "Auto Setter can kill bosses and monsters with **named presets, troop tiers, soloable lists, chaining and stamina rules**," the most solo-specific feature list of any cloud bot ([ESB Cloud](https://www.emulatorautomation.com/evony-cloud-bot)), and the vendor's 2026 explainer describes bots that "solo the bosses your marches can handle" ([ESB Blog](https://www.emulatorautomation.com/Blog/what-is-an-evony-bot)). The vendor has a track record here: its desktop ESB-TKR shipped a Monster Kill Mode that soloed regular monsters (not bosses) and auto-consumed stamina items up to 9,999 as early as 2022 ([ESB Monster Kill Guide](https://www.evonysmartbot.com/2022/02/monster-kill-guide.html)), with versioned releases continuing into 2024 ([ESB-TKR v2.5.3.5](https://www.evonysmartbot.com/2024/01/evony-smart-bot-esb-tkr-version-2535.html)). The snippets never define what "chaining" or "stamina rules" actually do, and no source lists which bosses or monster levels the cloud version can solo, so those are the first things to test.

The competitors document less. iScope's presets are "a full march recipe" (general, assistant general, troop composition and optional monster filter) that "fire round-robin" as each march returns. The feature is branded a Rally Setter, though, the snippets do not settle whether its kills are solo hits or rally starts, and iScope's own pages disagree on whether it comes free with 3+ account plans or costs $30 a month extra ([iScope blog](https://www.iscope.online/blog/evony-rally-setter); [iScope compare](https://auto.iscope.online/compare/iscout)). Evony Auto says it "attacks monsters" alongside map scans, building, research, training and dailies, but publishes no preset or monster-level controls ([Evony Auto](https://evonyauto.com/)). GNBots' cloud bot promises to "hunt monsters ... use stamina" and "manage marches" with no further detail ([GNBots Cloud](https://www.gnbots.com/auto/evony-cloud-bot/)). **EvonAI does not advertise soloing at all.** "Solo" is the name of its one-account price plan, and its pitch is rally joining and leading, a live map of every boss on the server, stamina top-ups and Discord spawn alerts ([EvonAI](https://evonai.app/)). ### Stamina turns "fastest" into "never stalls"

**Solo speed is mostly a stamina question.** Stamina regenerates about one point every 240 seconds, roughly 360 a day, but only while below 100, and several guides say it refills to 100 at the daily reset ([Theria Games stamina](https://theriagames.com/guide/evony-stamina-guide/); [EvonyGuru](https://www.evonyguru.com/monarch/stamina.html)). Bosses cost 15–20 stamina at levels 1–7 and up to 50 for a level-17 Typhon ([One Chilled Gamer](https://onechilledgamer.com/evony-boss-monster-guide/); [Evony Guide Wiki boss list](https://evonyguidewiki.com/en/boss-power-list-en/)). A fan wiki puts normal monsters at 6 stamina, though another source conflicts ([Fandom: Normal Monsters](https://evony-the-kings-return.fandom.com/wiki/Normal_Monsters)). On those figures, **natural regeneration funds roughly 75 normal-monster hits, 23–30 low-level bosses or 9–15 high-level bosses a day**, a budget that one or two marches can spend even though accounts top out at six march slots ([Theria march slots](https://theriagames.com/guide/march-slots-guide-evony/)). Past that, kills scale only with stamina items. The "fastest" bot is the one that never lets stamina sit at the cap, spends items by your rules without stalling, re-marches the moment a march returns, and avoids wounds. No public material separates the vendors on any of those four behaviours.

No bot calculates troop counts for you either. ESB, iScope and Ragebot all execute presets you build, and players size those presets with the Evony Guide Wiki's Boss Battle Simulator. A Junior Hydra, for example, needs 500,000+ T12 cavalry and goes zero-wound at 1 million, though wiki users report that simulator results do not always match real battles ([Boss Battle Simulator](https://evonyguidewiki.com/en/calculator-number-of-troops-to-kill-boss-en/); [How to kill Hydra](https://evonyguidewiki.com/en/how-to-kill-hydra-en/)). Mounted troops are the right tool, since ranged or ground troops need more than twice as many to do the same job ([Troop Type Guide](https://evonyguidewiki.com/?p=3714)). Target choice also beats raw speed: guides rank Azazel at about 400,000 RSS per stamina, Kraken about 320,000 and Ymir L3 about 267,000, with Cerberus L3 the best for speed-ups per stamina ([Theria bosses](https://theriagames.com/guide/bosses/); [EvonyTKRGuide boss rewards](https://www.evonytkrguide.com/guides/evony-tkr-guide-to-best-boss-rewards-rss-resources-speed-ups-comparison)). A bot pointed at the richest boss your march can kill without wounds out-earns a faster bot hitting whatever is nearest.

One structural limit applies to every cloud bot. **Evony allows one active session per account**, so a cloud bot hunting on your main pauses whenever you log in and resumes after you leave ([EvonAI](https://evonai.app/)). Cloud soloing on a main therefore runs only in your offline hours, and it places automation on the account you can least afford to lose. Ragebot's own manual adds a social cost: "most servers dislike players soloing large bosses" ([Ragebot Start Rallies](https://rage.systems/manual/startrallies.html)). For a cloud bot that solos with real control, trial ESB Cloud first. Choose Evony Auto if breadth across many accounts matters more than solo granularity, iScope only if its trial confirms solo hits, and Ragebot if you are willing to run an emulator yourself.

## Family Group and a level-30 Embassy set the RSS ceiling before any bot does

Moving RSS from alts to a main is now an officially sanctioned activity, and the rules that govern it live in the game, not the bot. Top Games' own documentation says Family Group members must share an alliance, must not have hit the daily transport limit, and must meet Embassy requirements, and that **"the Embassy level determines both the maximum number of daily transports and the maximum amount of resources that can be received"** ([Top Games: Family Group conditions](https://info.topgamesinc.com/docs/evonytkr/conditions-for-sharing-resources-within-a-family-group/)). The feature announcement declares that resource exchange between Family Group members, "regardless of the method," including transport, plundering and Royal Waybills, is **not a violation** ([Top Games: Family Group announcement](https://info.topgamesinc.com/evony-family-group-feature-announcement/)). Three caveats come with that. Transport fees still apply. Transported RSS lands **unprotected** on the main, where enemies can plunder it ([Top Games: conditions](https://info.topgamesinc.com/docs/evonytkr/conditions-for-sharing-resources-within-a-family-group/)). And Family Groups cannot be deleted, links cannot be removed, members cannot span servers, and the number of linked accounts depends on VIP level ([Theria Family Group guide](https://theriagames.com/guide/evony-family-group-guide/)).

The fee schedule makes the Embassy the single biggest lever. A roughly two-year-old community guide puts the transport fee at **60% at Embassy level 1, falling to 15% at level 30+** ([Theria Family Group guide](https://theriagames.com/guide/evony-family-group-guide/)), and higher Embassy levels also raise per-transport load and daily transport count ([Evony Guide Wiki: Embassy](https://evonyguidewiki.com/en/embassy-en/); [Theria Embassy guide](https://theriagames.com/guide/evony-embassy-guide/)). If the fee comes out of the shipment, a level-1 Embassy delivers 40 of every 100 units sent while a level-30 Embassy delivers 85, more than double. No official per-level table was found, so confirm the exact mechanics in game. The main's Embassy matters too, because it caps what the main can receive each day, and the Market must be upgraded before the Embassy ([Fandom: Embassy](https://evony-the-kings-return.fandom.com/wiki/Embassy)).

On the farm itself, community guidance is consistent. Max out farms, sawmills, quarries and mines, and raise the Keep only when a building unlock requires it ([AVTech farm guide](https://evony.avtechguides.com/evony-farm-account-leveling-guide/)). Gather the highest tile level your march can clear ([BlueStacks farming guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/evony-the-kings-return/etkr-resource-farming-guide-en.html)), and stack Queen of Silla (+20% gathered resources), the Jungle buff (+10% speed) and the gathering talent (+20%) ([MMO Culture](https://mmoculture.com/tips-guides/evony-the-kings-return-resource-optimization-guide/)). One guide says gathering can yield "upwards of three to four hundred million" resources a day without specifying the account setup ([EvonyTKRGuide gathering](https://www.evonytkrguide.com/guides/evony-tkr-guide-to-gathering-resources)), while a 2023 bot promo claims 28 billion a week, about ten times that figure and unverified ([BotBoss.4000 video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y4gfFQL_rz0)). The real multipliers are march count and 24/7 uptime, which is exactly what farm bots sell.

## Every bot breaks the ToS, and cloud trades password risk for session risk

The written rules leave no grey area. Search summaries of Evony's terms, reported as last updated August 2024 by one source and March 2025 by another, bar "cheats, exploits, **automation software, bots**, hacks, mods or any unauthorized third-party software" ([Evony Terms](https://m.evony.com/Terms.html); [Theria Games](https://theriagames.com/?p=8979)). The vendors concede the point: ESB Cloud's FAQ says bots break the ToS "on a PC or in the cloud ... the risk is yours," and ESB's 2026 explainer adds that "the game does ban accounts it believes are automated" ([ESB Cloud](https://www.emulatorautomation.com/evony-cloud-bot); [ESB Blog](https://www.emulatorautomation.com/Blog/what-is-an-evony-bot)). EvonAI and Evonex make similar admissions ([EvonAI](https://evonai.app/); [Evonex download](https://evonex.app/download)). **The sharpest documented penalty is not a ban but abandonment.** An official forum notice says Evony will not restore accounts "logged into with a bot," and warns that bots and helper programs "frequently capture account information" ([Evony forum: Account Security Reminder](https://bbs.evony.com/showthread.php?200074-Account-Security-Reminder&p=1840436)).

Enforcement evidence is thin and points both ways. An undated official forum thread records 7-day suspensions for suspected bot use, including players who say they were simply very active farmers ([Evony forum: Suspension for use of bots](https://bbs.evony.com/showthread.php?116345-Suspension-for-use-of-bots/page18&116345-Suspension-for-use-of-bots=&p=1081172)), and a Q&A page describes a 3-day rally ban after a bot report ([JustAnswer](https://www.justanswer.com/software/s9gqc-banned-evony-newd-know.html)). On the other side, Trustpilot reviewers say they reported 24/7 bot rally accounts on server 745 for over a year with no action ([Trustpilot: Top Games](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/topgamesinc.com)). No 2024–2026 ban wave was found. Vendor "never banned" claims from GNBots, Fbots and BoostBot are unverifiable ([GNBots TKR](https://www.gnbots.com/shop/evony-tkr-bot/); [Fbots](https://fbots.org/en/evony-bot); [BoostBot](https://boostbot.org/evony-kings-return-bot/)), and GNBots reviews include a reported "17 banned accounts" (game unclear) and a 2024 ban after using the free version ([GNBots Trustpilot p.9](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.gnbots.com?page=9)). Detection appears to key on behaviour, such as round-the-clock activity, which any 24/7 cloud bot produces regardless of design. A RAM/CPU-monitoring clause is documented only in the old browser-era Evony agreement, and the notes could not confirm it in current TKR terms ([Gaming Nexus](https://www.gamingnexus.com//FullNews/Slow-as-Sunday-Evony2c-Bruce2c-World-of-Warcraft2c-and-spyware/Item14685.aspx)).

## Free lossless screenshots beat paid parsers for PvP reports

TKR offers no documented way to export a battle report. **No source showed a TKR share URL, report ID or web viewer.** The "evonyurl" links and dxcalc calculator that players remember belong to the 2009-era browser Evony ([dxcalc](https://dxcalc.com/share/info.html); [Answers.com](https://www.answers.com/Q/How_do_you_upload_battle_reports_on_Evony_so_your_alliance_can_see_them)). What is documented is that mail "gets deleted frequently," so you should tap the star icon to save a report the moment a battle ends ([Evony TKR Player's Guide 4](https://evonyplayersguide.blogspot.com/p/beginner-tip-4-mail-chat-and-whisper.html)). A full report spans **main, buffs and details screens** ([Tactica: How it works](https://www.tactica-ai.com/how-it-works.html)), and only R4s and R5 can send alliance mail ([Evony TKR Player's Guide 5](https://evonyplayersguide.blogspot.com/p/beginner-tip-5-alliances.html)). Precision has a hard ceiling: the community wiki says buffs are "generally not shown in the battle report," so no capture method can recover a per-source buff breakdown the game never displays ([Evony Guide Wiki: Buff/Debuff guide](https://evonyguidewiki.com/en/buff_debuff_basic_guide-en/)).

The best free capture runs TKR on a large screen. The game is free on Windows through the Microsoft Store and Google Play Games for PC ([Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p2rh449lvl2?hl=en-US); [Google Play Games](https://play.google.com/pc-store/games/details?id=com.topgamesinc.evony)), and BlueStacks exposes resolution and DPI under Settings > Display ([BlueStacks DPI](https://support.bluestacks.com/hc/articles/4406352472461)). Raise the *resolution* for sharper text, because DPI changes UI density, not pixel count ([macmyths](https://macmyths.com/what-are-the-best-display-settings-for-bluestacks-5/)). Save **PNG, not JPEG**, one image per screen, with overlapping shots down the details list. Phones are a poor fit: Android and iOS scroll capture does not engage in games ([Mezha](https://mezha.media/en/articles/how-to-take-a-long-scrolling-screenshot-on-android-and-ios)). On Windows, the free, open-source **ShareX** offers scrolling capture that stitches frames, and version 17.0 (January 2025) improved it; it stops automatically if stitching fails, so a failed attempt costs nothing, but nobody has confirmed it works on TKR's details screen ([ShareX docs](https://getsharex.com/docs/scrolling-screenshot); [ShareX changelog](https://GetShareX.com/changelog); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/1/sharex-17-0-released-with-enhanced-scrolling-capture-notification-sounds-and-much-more/)).

**Tactica** is the only current tool found that turns TKR reports into structured data. Its Windows app drives an Android emulator through each report's three screens, or pulls starred reports directly with "Fast Read" (MuMu only). It stores troop layers, generals, buffs and timestamps, but **"the on-disk format is proprietary. There is no CSV or JSON dump"**, only copyable tables from its Analyst ([Tactica FAQ](https://www.tactica-ai.com/faq.html); [Tactica: How it works](https://www.tactica-ai.com/how-it-works.html)). It costs $14.99 a month after a 7-day trial, with view-only alliance seats at $2.49 ([Tactica download](https://www.tactica-ai.com/download.html)). No independent accuracy review exists, and because it automates the game client over ADB it carries the same ToS exposure as any bot. Pay for it only if your alliance needs bulk battlefield analysis; for everyday sharing, the free PNG-plus-Sheet workflow is more precise per dollar. Whatever you use, follow Tactica's own etiquette and do not post other players' identifiable reports without consent ([Tactica cookbook](https://www.tactica-ai.com/cookbook.html)).

## Make the free trials do the proving

Since public evidence cannot rank these bots, your own 24–48-hour trials are the benchmark. Because Evony permits one session per account, test each bot on a *different* farm account, or run them one after another, never two on the same account at once. Record the same four numbers for every trial: kills per stamina spent, wounded troops per day, RSS gathered per day, and RSS actually landed on the main after fees.

## Conclusion

Asking which bot is best puts the question in the wrong place for this market. Rally joining and gathering are commodities that every vendor sells. The features you care about, granular solo presets and automated transfer to the main, are the least documented, and the real ceilings are set by the game (stamina regeneration, Embassy fees, Family Group caps) and by trust (who holds a live session to your account). A bot cannot solo faster than your stamina refills or move RSS beyond your Embassy's limits, so a careful setup with a mid-tier bot will beat a "premium" bot on a badly configured account.

The Family Group change also reshapes the risk picture. Transfers between linked accounts are now explicitly legitimate, which leaves the automation itself as the main exposure. The cleanest strategy is to bot the farms, play the main by hand or accept its risk knowingly, and treat your own trial measurements as the only benchmark that exists. For battle reports, the same principle holds: the game's display is the precision ceiling, so a free, disciplined PNG workflow captures everything any paid parser can.
